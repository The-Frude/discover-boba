This Cookie Policy explains how DiscoverBoba.com uses cookies and other tracking technologies to improve your experience.

## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function properly, remember your preferences, and gather analytics.

## 2. How We Use Cookies
We use cookies to:
- Enable basic website functionality
- Remember your preferences (e.g., city or filters)
- Analyze traffic using tools like Google Analytics
- Show relevant ads through Google AdSense

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use
- **Essential Cookies**: Required for core functionality
- **Performance Cookies**: Help us understand usage patterns
- **Functional Cookies**: Remember user choices
- **Advertising Cookies**: Help deliver more relevant ads

## 4. Managing Cookies
You can modify your browser settings to block or delete cookies. However, doing so may affect how our site functions.

## 5. Third-Party Cookies
Some cookies may be placed by third-party services (like Google or social platforms). We don't control these cookies, and their policies apply.
